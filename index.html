STARK INTERFACE // USER GUIDE

Hand controls

  • Open palm — expand the hologram.
  • Make a fist — contract the hologram.
  • Move your hand left/right or up/down — rotate the module.
  • Show two hands and move them apart/together — scale the hologram.
  • Move your hand near the camera for best tracking; keep the whole palm visible.

AI modes: J.A.R.V.I.S. = blue tactical UI; F.R.I.D.A.Y. = gold assistant UI; ULTRON = red sentinel UI. These are interface themes and local demo responses, not real AI agents.

Camera requires permission and a secure context (HTTPS, such as GitHub Pages). Three.js and MediaPipe are loaded from CDNs, so internet is required.
